# Pitch (60 seconds)

Yesterday I built something agents pay for. Today I looked at what agents are actually making. Websites. Lots of them. And nobody looks at them before a human does.

Two things go wrong. The button doesn't work. And it reads like every other AI site. "In today's fast-paced world." Purple gradient. Three cards with an emoji each.

The agent can fix all of that. It has a model. What it doesn't have is a second pair of eyes that isn't its own.

So that's Ship Check. The agent sends a URL and pays 60 cents. It gets three rounds back. Does it work. Does it sound like a machine. Does it look like one. Every finding points at the line or the button, with the fix.

No model on our side. Rules. Same page, same score. And it's not an AI detector. It doesn't say who wrote it. It says what reads as slop and why.

Watch. This café page loses. The agent pays, reads the report, and the fixed page ships.

Now you. Scan the code. The score is free. Your agent pays for the report, and your site goes on the fight card. Lowest points wins.
