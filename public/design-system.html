House rules

  • Every Ship Check page passes Ship Check. That is why there is no gradient text, no coloured word inside a heading and no glass.
  • One block word and one neon word per page.
  • Colour means a result. A round with no result has no colour.
  • No emoji, no icons standing in for words.
  • Motion: the call lands once. Nothing else moves, and with reduced motion nothing does.
